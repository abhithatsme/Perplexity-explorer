Mathematical Computation Steps

Step-by-step reduction from individual token reciprocal probabilities to the scalar perplexity score.

STEP 1
Surprise Product
2 × 1.25 × 5 = 12.5

Multiply reciprocal probabilities $(1/P_i)$ across all tokens in the sequence.

STEP 2
Token Count Normalization
N = 3 tokens

Count sequence length $N$ to normalize uncertainty per token.

STEP 3
Geometric Mean (Perplexity)
(12.5)1/3 = 2.321

Take the $N$-th root to yield the final scalar perplexity.

Why Perplexity is the Golden Benchmark in LLMs

When training frontier models (like GPT-4, LLaMA, or Gemini), training loss directly optimizes cross-entropy, which exponentiates into perplexity. A model with lower perplexity has captured the syntactic and semantic structure of human language with higher statistical precision.
